# Privacy Policy — Manage Multiple Social Accounts by Shahroz Malik

**Last updated: August 14, 2026**

## Overview
Manage Multiple Social Accounts ("the Extension") is developed by Shahroz Malik / Auralix Studio. This privacy policy explains how the Extension handles user data.

## Data Collection
The Extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:
- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Passwords or authentication credentials
- Financial or payment information
- Health information
- Location data
- Browsing history or web activity

## Local Storage
The Extension stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- Draft content (title, description, hashtags) to avoid data loss between sessions
- Extension settings and platform preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any external server.

## Backend Services
The Extension communicates with Google Apps Script solely for license verification and user authentication purposes. No personal content or social media data is transmitted.

## Third-Party Platforms
The Extension interacts with Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn to auto-fill upload fields. We do not access, read, or store any data from these platforms.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
